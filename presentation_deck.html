⚠ The Industrial Problem Statement

Conventional quality inspection in casting foundries relies overwhelmingly on manual visual inspection by human operators. This legacy paradigm suffers from severe systemic vulnerabilities:

High Defect Escape Rate & Operator Fatigue:

Repetitive shifts cause up to 15-20% escape rates on microscopic surface flaws, pinholes, blowholes, and hairline perimeter cracks.

Subjective & Disconnected Judgments:

Inconsistent criteria between shifts, zero optical evidence capture, and no traceability back to batch melting or molding heat numbers.

Broken Remediation Loops:

Defects are isolated in paper logs without structured 8D Corrective & Preventive Action (CAPA) tracking or root cause synthesis.

• Closed-Loop Tri-Role Governance: Integrated Inspector → Supervisor → Quality Manager sign-off workflow with audit trails and CAPA tracking.

🔄 End-to-End Operational Data Lifecycle Flow

1. Part Optical Capture & Validation

Industrial camera captures casting component. Client validates MIME type (PNG/JPEG), enforces <8MB payload, and applies deterministic SHA-256 fingerprinting.

2. High-Speed Tensor Transformation

Image standardized to 128×128 RGB, transformed via ImageNet mean/variance vectors, and ingested into memory buffer with zero disk-write bottlenecks during live screening.

3. Dual-Stream Inference & Explainability

PyTorch CNN computes calibrated defect posterior probabilities. Simultaneous backward gradient hook extracts Block 4 activations to render the Grad-CAM thermal overlay in ~12ms.

4. Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Disposition

Operator evaluates optical capture side-by-side with Grad-CAM heatmap. Final disposition (Good / Defective / Quarantine) recorded with human sign-off note.

5. Closed-Loop 8D Remediation & Audit Logging

Flagged defects trigger mandatory 8D CAPA assignments. Every action, state transition, and supervisor override is committed into the immutable SQLite audit ledger.

⚙ Production Algorithms & TTA

Test-Time Augmentation (TTA):

Borderline samples undergo 5-pass stochastic inference (random rotation, flips, color jitter). Probabilities are averaged to prevent false rejections.

Grad-CAM Backpropagation Hook:

Gradients of the target defect class logit are pooled across Block 4 feature maps to compute channel importance weights αkc in sub-10ms.

Thermal Heatmap Alpha-Blending:

Activation tensor normalized with OpenCV/PIL JET colormap, interpolated to 128×128, and blended at 40% opacity with the original grayscale item.
