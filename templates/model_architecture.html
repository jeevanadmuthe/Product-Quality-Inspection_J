Deep Learning Conceptual Hierarchy

Unlike classical SVM with fixed handcrafted HOG descriptors, the CNN learns optimal feature representations hierarchically.

Active Model
Stage 1 · Input 128x128x3
Raw RGB Casting Image
Standardized RGB tensor normalized with ImageNet statistical mean and variance vectors.
Stage 2 · Low-Level 64x64x32
Conv Block 1 (Dual 3x3)
Captures microscopic surface transitions, metallic specular contrast, and high-contrast boundaries.
Stage 3 · Mid-Level 32x32x64
Conv Block 2 (Dual 3x3)
Extracts casting grain roughness, tool marks, and localized circular porosity candidates.
Stage 4 · High-Level 8x8x256
Conv Blocks 3 & 4
Synthesizes macro casting defects: blowholes, shrinkage cavities, cold laps, and perimeter fractures.
Stage 5 · Classifier 2 Classes
Adaptive Pool + Dense Head
Spatial pooling provides shift invariance; dense head with Dropout (0.35) computes Softmax class probabilities.
